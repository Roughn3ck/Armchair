> *"Ave. I am Agricola, commander of the Roman forces. State your business."*

## Adding More Voices

Download from [Piper Voices](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/blob/master/VOICES.md) and place the `.onnx` and `.onnx.json` files in your Piper models directory.
